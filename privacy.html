Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

In short

Your audio, lyrics, images and videos are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to our servers. We only store data if you create an account (your email and your plan). We use no advertising or third-party analytics, and you can delete your account at any time from within the app.

01Who is responsible

Lyric Studio (“the app”) is developed and operated by Mirage Development, run by Valentín Pinto, located in Argentina. For any question about this policy, you can email us: Support@lyricstudio.site.

02What happens to your files

The audio, lyrics (.lrc, .srt or pasted text), images, background videos and fonts you load are read and processed locally in your browser. The final video is also generated on your device. None of this is sent to our servers, and we neither see it nor store it.

The projects you save (.lyr) are downloaded as a file to your computer. They contain the lyrics, timings and style and also the audio, images, videos and fonts you used in the project, so keep these files private if that material is not meant to be shared.

03Data stored in your browser

So you don’t lose your work, the app uses your browser’s local storage (localStorage) to save:

  • an autosave of your current project (lyrics, timings and style);
  • the styles you save as presets;
  • your account session, if you are signed in.

This data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser settings.

Desktop app. In the desktop version, your projects are saved automatically in your computer’s application data folder (on Windows, inside %APPDATA%), including copies of the audio, images and videos you used. They are not uploaded to our servers. You can delete a project from the project list on the home screen (it moves to the Recycle Bin) or by deleting its folder.

05Payments

The Pro plan is paid once (a one-time payment: it is not a subscription and there are no recurring charges) and the payment is processed by PayPal. We do not receive or store your card or PayPal account details; we only receive confirmation that the payment was made, so we can activate your plan. PayPal’s handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy.

06Third-party services

To work, the app relies on these providers:

  • Supabase: hosts the account system and the database where your email and plan are stored.
  • Google: its sign-in script is loaded only when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • PayPal: its payment script is loaded only when you are about to pay.

These providers may process data on servers outside your country and receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type. The app includes no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

07Cookies

The app does not set its own advertising or tracking cookies. As explained above, it uses local storage for autosave, styles and your session. Google and PayPal may set their own cookies when you use their services within the app.

08How long we keep your data

We keep your account data for as long as the account exists. If you delete it, the data associated with it is removed. The only data we keep after that is the record of your payment (transaction number, date and amount), for as long as the law requires us to retain it.

09Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Delete your account: do it yourself from the app, in your account menu, using the delete account option. Deleting your account does not erase the payment record described in section 08.
  • Any other request: write to us at the contact email and we will respond as soon as possible.

Because Mirage Development operates from Argentina, this policy is governed by Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection. You may exercise the right of access free of charge at intervals of not less than six months, unless you prove a legitimate interest. The Agency for Access to Public Information, as the supervisory authority under that law, handles complaints from people who believe their rights have been affected.

10Security

Connections to our services use HTTPS, and access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules, so each person can only access their own data. No system is completely infallible, but we apply reasonable measures to protect your information.

11Age and purchases

You can use the Lyric Studio editor at any age and without an account. To create an account you must be at least 13 years old (or the age required in your country to consent to the processing of your data, if it is higher). To pay for the Pro plan you must be over 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with its update date. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know within the app.
